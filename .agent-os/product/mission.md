# Product Mission

> Last Updated: 2025-08-02
> Version: 1.0.0

## Pitch

PsychEval AI is a SAAS platform that helps psychiatrists and clinical psychologists transform patient interviews into comprehensive, standards-compliant psychiatric evaluations by providing seamless audio recording, multi-language transcription, and AI-powered documentation generation.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Psychiatrists**: Medical doctors specializing in mental health who need efficient, accurate documentation while maintaining clinical standards
- **Clinical Psychologists**: Licensed mental health professionals who require comprehensive evaluation reports for treatment planning

### User Personas

**Dr. Sarah Chen** (35-45 years old)
- **Role:** Psychiatrist in private practice
- **Context:** Sees 8-12 patients daily, spends 2-3 hours on documentation
- **Pain Points:** Time-consuming documentation, maintaining consistency across evaluations, ensuring compliance with standards
- **Goals:** Reduce documentation time by 70%, maintain high-quality evaluations, focus more on patient care

**Dr. Miguel Rodriguez** (40-50 years old)
- **Role:** Clinical Psychologist at community mental health center
- **Context:** Works with diverse patient population, many Spanish-speaking clients
- **Pain Points:** Language barriers in documentation, lengthy evaluation process, administrative burden
- **Goals:** Accurate multi-language transcription, standardized evaluations, improved workflow efficiency

## The Problem

### Documentation Burden in Mental Health

Mental health professionals spend 40-60% of their time on documentation, reducing patient care time. Manual note-taking during sessions can disrupt therapeutic rapport and miss important clinical details.

**Our Solution:** Automated recording and AI-powered evaluation generation that captures the full clinical encounter.

### Language Barriers in Clinical Practice

Many clinicians work with multilingual patients but struggle to accurately document sessions conducted in multiple languages. This leads to incomplete or inaccurate clinical records.

**Our Solution:** Multi-language transcription supporting English, Filipino, Spanish, and other languages through OpenAI Whisper.

### Consistency in Clinical Documentation

Maintaining consistent, standards-compliant psychiatric evaluations across different patients and sessions is challenging. This inconsistency can impact treatment quality and insurance reimbursement.

**Our Solution:** AI-generated evaluations following established psychiatric evaluation standards, ensuring consistency and compliance.

## Differentiators

### Standards-Based AI Generation

Unlike generic medical transcription services, we generate evaluations specifically following established psychiatric evaluation standards. This results in documentation that meets professional requirements while maintaining the patient-centered, recovery-oriented approach outlined in clinical best practices.

### Multi-Language Clinical Intelligence

Unlike competitors limited to English, we provide accurate transcription and analysis for multilingual clinical sessions. This enables clinicians to serve diverse populations without compromising documentation quality.

### Integrated Clinical Workflow

Unlike standalone transcription tools, we provide an end-to-end solution from recording through final evaluation. This seamless integration reduces administrative burden by 70% while improving documentation quality.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Secure Audio Recording:** HIPAA-compliant recording of clinical sessions with automatic backup
- **Multi-Language Transcription:** Accurate transcription in English, Filipino, Spanish, and other languages via OpenAI Whisper
- **AI-Powered Evaluation Generation:** Standards-compliant psychiatric evaluations generated using Claude Sonnet 4 API
- **Real-Time Processing:** Begin reviewing evaluations immediately after session completion

### Collaboration Features

- **Clinician Review Interface:** Edit and refine AI-generated evaluations with track changes
- **Template Management:** Customize evaluation templates while maintaining standards compliance
- **Export Options:** Generate reports in multiple formats (PDF, Word, EMR-compatible)
- **Secure Sharing:** Share evaluations with authorized healthcare providers
- **Audit Trail:** Complete documentation history for compliance and quality assurance
